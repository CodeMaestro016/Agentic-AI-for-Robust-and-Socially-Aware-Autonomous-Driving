Academic Research · R26-DS-017

Agentic AI for Robust and Socially-Aware Autonomous Driving

Sri Lanka Institute of Information Technology
Data Science Research Project

This project investigates pedestrian intent under occlusion, decentralized right-of-way negotiation, pedestrian behavioral-state prediction, and selective traffic-sign knowledge sharing. Four independently developed studies use task-specific datasets and simulation-based evaluation to examine bounded research feasibility and its limitations.

Explore Research ↗ View Results Research Documents

Four research components

Independent studies presented under one broader academic project.

Component 01

Adaptive-Intent Prediction in Occluded Urban Scenarios

Occlusion-aware pedestrian intention prediction using reliability, uncertainty, and a selective policy.

Explore component →

Component 02

Multi-Agent Negotiation for Right-of-Way in Complex Intersections

Decentralized right-of-way negotiation using ego-local information and rule-constrained MAPPO; dense-traffic liveness remains limited.

Explore component →

Component 03

Proactive Mental State Prediction Modeling using Theory of Mind

Six operational pedestrian behavioral categories and rule-based TTC risk-aware decision support.

Explore component →

Component 04

Agentic Knowledge Sharing for Federated Collective Learning in Autonomous Vehicles

Selective verified traffic-sign knowledge sharing with separate local classifiers and later retraining.

Explore component →

About this website

This academic information website documents the project domain, research methods, findings, assessments, documents, and team. The research prototypes are separate deliverables; the four algorithms are not presented as a tightly coupled production driving system.
