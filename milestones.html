Research Project Milestones

Key research assessments and milestones of Group R26-DS-017 during the 2026 academic year.

01 Project Proposal Presentation & Report 16 March 2026 12% Completed

Presented the research problem, identified research gaps, defined project objectives, and introduced the proposed methodology and solution.

Allocated assessment weighting: 12%.

Key Panel Feedback

  • Fairness and Time-Based Evaluation

    The panel emphasized the importance of incorporating fairness and time-related measurements into the evaluation of the overall research project.

  • Research Scope Refinement

    For the occlusion and pedestrian-intention research component, the panel recommended narrowing the research scope to selected urban scenarios.

    For the multi-agent right-of-way negotiation component, the panel recommended focusing on selected complex intersections rather than addressing overly broad traffic environments.

02 Progress Presentation 1 (PP1) 12 May 2026 15% Completed

Presented the initial research development, implementation progress, individual component approaches, and preliminary technical work.

Allocated assessment weighting: 15%.

Key Panel Feedback

  • Research Background and Technical Understanding

    The panel requested a stronger understanding of the research background and the selected topic across the individual research components.

  • System Demonstration Planning

    The team was advised to plan how the implemented systems would be demonstrated effectively during subsequent presentations.

  • Complex Scenario Evaluation

    For the multi-agent right-of-way negotiation component, the panel specifically requested that more complex scenarios be addressed by Progress Presentation 2.

  • Terminology and Research Positioning

    For the traffic-sign knowledge-sharing component, the panel recommended reviewing whether the term “Federated Learning” accurately described the proposed approach.

03 Progress Presentation 2 (PP2) 1 September 2026 18% Completed

Demonstrated further system development, more advanced implementation and research progress, and experimental evaluation of the individual components.

Allocated assessment weighting: 18%.

Key Panel Feedback

  • Component 1 — Occlusion-Aware Intention Prediction

    The panel recommended increasing the number and variety of test cases to demonstrate the capabilities and strengths of the system more thoroughly.

  • Component 2 — Multi-Agent Right-of-Way Negotiation

    The panel observed that the demonstration involved only four vehicles at a cross junction.

    It recommended evaluating the system under a more congested traffic scenario to provide stronger evidence of its behaviour in complex intersection conditions.

  • Component 3 — Pedestrian Behaviour Prediction

    The panel observed that the demonstration involved one pedestrian and recommended extending evaluation to situations involving multiple pedestrians.

  • Component 4 — Traffic-Sign Knowledge Sharing

    The panel requested testing with a wider variety of traffic symbols to strengthen the evaluation.

04 Website 11 October 2026 2% Completed

Submission of the academic research information website documenting the research domain, project milestones, methodologies, findings, publications and project team.

Allocated assessment weighting: 2%.

05 Research Logbook & Status Documents 19–21 October 2026 4% Completed

Submission and evaluation of research progress records, supervisor meeting documentation and required project status documents.

Allocated assessment weighting: 4%.

06 Final Presentation & Viva 20 October 2026 20% Completed

Presentation and academic defence of the completed research, including methodologies, system demonstrations, experimental findings, individual contributions and limitations.

Allocated assessment weighting: 20%.

07 Research Paper Submission deadline: 23 October 2026 10% Completed

Submission and publication-related documentation of the individual research contributions, methodologies and findings.

Allocated assessment weighting: 10% — Research Paper (Published) category.

Publication evidence deadline: 1 December 2026.

08 Final Report Submission deadline: 28 October 2026 19% Upcoming

Submission of the completed individual research theses and common group report, presenting research methodology, implementation, evaluation, results, discussion and conclusions.

Allocated assessment weighting: 19%.

Assessment percentages represent allocated module weightings, not marks obtained. Weightings are based on the supplied IT4010 module outline (version 2019-0).
